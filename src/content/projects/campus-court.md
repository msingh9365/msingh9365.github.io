## Overview

Campus Court lets students at IIT Ropar book sports facilities from their phone and see which slots are free right now.

## How it works

- **Flutter** Android app for students.
- **Django** backend with cloud-hosted REST APIs.
- **Redis** for live slot availability.
- **Supabase** for hosted data.
